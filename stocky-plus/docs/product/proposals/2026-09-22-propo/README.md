## 4. Product priorities

1. Preserve parity, variant identity, receiving correctness, reconciliation and useful exports.
2. Capture the minimum versioned operational history needed for future evaluation without unnecessary customer PII.
3. Pilot a small number of coherent decision policies rather than every toggle combination.
4. Add optimization only after reliable costs, lead times, price breaks and budget commitments are available.
5. Add integrations because pilot merchants need a specific data flow, not to enlarge a logo wall.

Do not market guaranteed profit, no stockouts, worldwide legal compliance, “unlimited AI,” or superiority over every inventory app. The approved pricing architecture keeps essential workflows on every paid plan; additional evaluation and numerical computation are not automatically LLM-credit events. Validate infrastructure/support economics and price hypotheses with pilots.

## 5. Research and validation plan — not yet executed

Desk research reviewed official Shopify/Apple/W3C/regulator material, primary forecasting sources and vendor integration pages (sources R01–R24). Vendor pages establish advertised features only. No survey prevalence, actual adoption, connector reliability, measured ROI or competitor deficiency is inferred from marketing.

Proposed interviews/usability work: 6–10 merchants spanning single-store POS, multi-location, seasonal retail and long-tail inventory; include buyers, receivers and owners, not owners only. This is directional discovery, not a statistically representative survey. Use consented/synthetic data and tasks: find replenishment need, explain a quantity, exclude a discontinued item, fit a tight budget, send a PO, receive partially, recover failure and find prior evidence.

Record baseline task time, task completion, misorders caught, quantity overrides/reasons, unexplained recommendations, receiving discrepancies, support burden, return to use and willingness to pay. Backtest forecasts chronologically and separately run stock/control simulations. A measured forecast error improvement is not automatically realized cash or profit improvement.
